## Projects (AIF lists 2)
**P1: "Hallam Road deep basement: innovative retaining solution in challenging ground conditions."** A two-storey basement under a Victorian terrace, with a high water table and made ground. The solution was a secant pile wall with a temporary propping sequence, *designed by Pennine Structural Consultants (an external engineer engaged on behalf of the client, the homeowner's design team)*. Brackenmoor built it.
- The narrative presents the "uncertainty" as "whether the adjacent party wall would remain stable during excavation" and "unknown ground conditions". This is a standard engineering risk, managed with monitoring to established practice (Collins Construction style).
- The claimed "advance" is "the Company's enhanced capability in deep basement construction".
- The contract was a JCT design-and-build variant, paid by the client at a fixed price of £1.38m. Mention it in background as the company's "largest residential basement to date". This is a flag: the client funded the work, and the work was routine.
- Materials incorporated into the building (concrete, piles, rebar: £212k) are claimed as consumables. Plant hire is also in consumables.

**P2: "Digital transformation of site operations."** A cloud-based site diary app, drone surveys and a BIM viewer, adopted from vendors. The uncertainty is "whether site teams would adopt the technology".
