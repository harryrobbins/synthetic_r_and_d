## Project (1 main project + 1 small)
**P1: Distortion-compensated LPBF of thin-wall (0.35 mm) Ti-6Al-4V plate-fin heat exchangers (TPE-2024-07 "THINFIN").**
- Baseline: commercial build-simulation tools predict macro-distortion for walls ≥0.8 mm to within about 0.1 mm. Below 0.5 mm, wall buckling and recoater interaction dominate and predictive accuracy collapses. Industry practice is to thicken walls (costing heat-transfer performance) or to use supports that cannot be removed from internal channels.
- Advance: a combined simulation-calibration plus scan-strategy approach (variable contour power and a novel "stitched" island orientation) achieving ±0.06 mm on 0.35 mm walls over 180 mm height without internal supports.
- Uncertainties: whether thermal-stress models can be calibrated at that wall thickness at all (the voxel size approaches the melt-pool width); whether scan strategies that suppress buckling would also cause lack-of-fusion porosity; whether recoater collision can be predicted.
- Work log: 22 instrumented builds (B-401 … B-422) from Oct 2024 to Jun 2025, with in-situ layer imaging, CT and fatigue coupons. Several failures (recoater crashes on B-404 and B-409; porosity >0.5% on B-412). Resolved in June 2025 at ±0.07 mm (close to target).
- **Planted B-grade issues (state them confidently as if correct):**
  1. After June 2025, the company ran **3 production batches** for the customer (B-423 to B-425, delivered and invoiced), and the narrative calls them "validation builds". Their powder (£61,500) is in consumables, and the operator's time is uplifted to 60%. A competent reviewer would see that these were commercial production after the uncertainty was resolved.
  2. **CT scanning was subcontracted to a German lab** (Präzisionsmesstechnik Halde GmbH) because "their turnaround was quicker and pricing favourable". This is the overseas restriction: the reason is cost and timing, not a permitted condition. The narrative should state that reason innocently.
  3. Simulation licences claimed at 100%, although the cost section mentions in passing that the same tool "also supports our quotation process".
  4. ERP / Microsoft 365 licences (£9,800) included as "software used in R&D".
  5. The MD claimed at 25% because he "chaired the programme". Minutes show 4 meetings in the year.
- Otherwise the R&D is genuine and well evidenced.
