## Projects (AIF lists 2)
**P1: Iridium loading below 0.2 mg/cm² at the anode with <10 µV/h degradation at 2 A/cm² ("QH-LOWIR").**
- Baseline: commercial PEM anodes use 1–2 mg/cm² iridium. Published low-loading approaches (Ir on doped-TiO₂ supports; thin-film nanostructured catalysts) show degradation >30 µV/h under dynamic load because of support dissolution and loss of PTL contact.
- Advance: a graded catalyst layer on a coated porous transport layer, with interface engineering keeping contact resistance stable.
- Uncertainties: the dissolution mechanism under the load-cycling profiles typical of renewables; whether the PTL coating survives; the ionomer-catalyst interaction at low loading.
- Work: 36 MEA builds (MEA-001…036), 14,000 cumulative test hours, and accelerated stress tests (AST) at a Dutch facility (Stichting Waterstof Testlab). A failure mode (Ti PTL passivation) was identified in May 2025 and resolved with a Nb-doped coating in Sept 2025. Not yet fully resolved: by Dec 2025, 6.8 µV/h at 0.18 mg/cm² over 2,000 h.
- Excellent technical detail.

**P2: Stack-level thermal management for dynamic operation ("QH-DYNA").** A thinner project (about 1,500 words), but genuine.

## Planted issues (technical, not narrative)
1. **Overseas subcontractor:** the Dutch AST lab costs £120k × 65% = £78k. The narrative says the Dutch facility was used "because it is the only facility able to run our bespoke AST protocol at the required scale with immediate availability, and UK alternatives would have delayed the programme by 6 months". This is availability and time, not a permitted geographic, environmental or legal condition. Write it as a confident justification.
2. **ERIS intensity manipulation:** section 06 includes an intensity calculation showing relevant R&D expenditure of £715,519 over total relevant expenditure of £2,250,000 = 31.8%. It footnotes that "pilot plant operating costs (£420,000) are excluded from total expenditure as they relate to a separate demonstration activity". This is wrong: these are company expenditure and belong in the denominator. A careful reviewer recomputes about 23.5% after also removing the overseas cost. Show the calculation table.
3. The Commercial Director is claimed at 10% ("technical customer requirement gathering").
4. The grant is disclosed correctly. State that it does not restrict the claim under the post-April-2024 rules; that statement is correct.
