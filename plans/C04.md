- 74 staff, turnover £6.2m, profitable.
- Agent: Quorlane R&D Partners Ltd, a no-win-no-fee boutique taking 25% of the benefit. **The same agent as C05 and C06.** Its signature boilerplate must appear verbatim in all three packs. Use this exact sentence in section 05's introduction:
  > "Throughout the claim period the Company sought to achieve an appreciable improvement in the overall knowledge and capability in its field, resolving uncertainties that could not be readily deduced by a competent professional working in the field, in accordance with paragraphs 3, 6 and 13 of the DSIT Guidelines."

  Also this AIF closing line: *"All activities were undertaken in a systematic, iterative and controlled manner, with outcomes recorded contemporaneously."*
- Senior officer: Isla Hollin, MD. Competent professional: **Marcus Rye, Executive Chef** (25 years of culinary experience, a Michelin Bib Gourmand at a previous restaurant, no scientific qualifications). The narrative calls him "a recognised expert in culinary science".
- **First claim, but a CNF was filed** (on 22 Aug 2025). Section 02 should reproduce it. Valid.

## Projects (AIF lists 3)
**P1: "Development of novel fermentation-led flavour systems for a seasonal tasting menu."** Koji, garums and lacto-fermented vegetables, developed by trial and error across 4 seasonal menus. The uncertainty is described as "whether diners would accept bold umami-forward profiles" and "achieving consistent flavour across batches". The baseline is described only as "traditional restaurant techniques". Narrative is full of sensory language, with dish names and dates of menu launches.

**P2: "Advanced sous-vide protein texturisation programme."** Time and temperature trials for short rib and octopus, a technique that is well documented. Equipment purchased (£22.5k: water baths, a chamber vacuum sealer, a fermentation chamber) is claimed as consumables.

**P3: "Shelf-life extension for chilled retail range."** *This is the one borderline-qualifying element.* A food technologist (Ella Brandt, BSc Food Science) worked with an external microbiology lab on a combination of modified-atmosphere packaging and a natural antimicrobial (cultured dextrose) to extend a chilled braised dish from 5 to 12 days. It still mostly applies established hurdle technology. Write about 1,000 words with some real data (challenge tests for *Listeria*, water activity, pH). This is the strongest part of the claim.

## Planted issues
- The whole kitchen brigade (14 FTE) is claimed at 25% for "participating in menu trials".
- Head chefs are at 50%. The Executive Chef is at 60%, and the MD at 30% "for strategic direction of the R&D programme".
- Ingredients for dishes that were then served to paying customers are claimed as consumables (£64k).
- Capital equipment is in consumables.
- The "evidence" is menus, Instagram posts, tasting notes and staff WhatsApp group exports. Reference them in the evidence index (e.g. "HR-EV-031: Instagram post 14 Oct 2024 announcing autumn menu").
- The section 05 intro and AIF use the Quorlane boilerplate verbatim (see above).
- Business uncertainties throughout ("whether dishes would be commercially viable", "managing food cost margins").
