### Projects claimed

The company carried out two research and development projects in the period. Both are described in full in section 05.

1. **CVB-P1 "LONGFLOW"**: multipoint covalent immobilisation of an engineered ω-transaminase, retaining more than 80% activity over 500 hours in continuous flow. This is the principal project. It continues the feasibility work carried out in the year to 31 March 2024 and is claimed from 1 April 2024.
2. **CVB-P2 "PREDICTA"**: a computational surrogate model for predicting activity retained by an enzyme after covalent immobilisation. The project did not meet its primary performance target, and it continues.

### Cost categories

The full schedules are in section 06.

### Basis of the claim

The company's R&D addresses a technical problem in low-temperature biocatalysis for continuous-flow pharmaceutical manufacture. The work was carried out in the United Kingdom, at the company's laboratory in Calderbridge, under the direction of a competent professional with a doctorate in enzyme engineering. The advance sought, the uncertainties faced, and the systematic approach taken to resolve them are set out in the Additional Information Form (section 03) and in the technical narrative (section 05).

The company has excluded from this claim costs of £61,000 relating to customer feasibility studies, GMP documentation work, and stock drawn for those activities. Those exclusions are explained in section 06.
