### 7.1 Competent professional curriculum vitae: Dr Nadia Fenwick

**Name:** Dr Nadia Fenwick
**Position:** Chief Technology Officer, Quillon Hydrogen Ltd (from 2021)
**Contact:** through the Company, Building 4, Tyneford Energy Park, Wallsend Road, Tyneford ZZ28 6QJ

#### Qualifications

- PhD in Electrochemistry, University of Harwick, 2011. Thesis on the kinetics of oxygen evolution at mixed-metal oxide anodes in acidic media.
- MSc in Chemical Engineering with Electrochemical Technology, University of Harwick, 2007.
- BSc (Hons) Chemistry, University of Kestrel, 2006.

#### Employment history

- **2021 to present:** Co-founder and Chief Technology Officer, Quillon Hydrogen Ltd. Responsible for the technical strategy of the Company, the design and test programme for PEM electrolyser stacks, and the supervision of the R&D team.
- **2019 to 2021:** Research Lead, Stack Development, Velden Stackwerk GmbH, Germany. Led a team of nine engineers and scientists working on the durability and thermal management of PEM stacks for industrial hydrogen production. Responsible for the stack durability programme and for the transfer of laboratory results into the production design.
- **2014 to 2019:** Senior Research Scientist, Velden Stackwerk GmbH. Worked on catalyst layer design, membrane electrode assembly fabrication and electrochemical characterisation of fuel cell and electrolyser components.
- **2011 to 2014:** Postdoctoral Research Fellow, University of Harwick. Worked on the electrochemistry of iridium-based catalysts for acidic water electrolysis.

#### Experience relevant to this claim

Dr Fenwick has twelve years' experience in fuel cells and electrolysers. Her experience includes the design and testing of MEAs at low catalyst loading, the characterisation of catalyst layers and porous transport layers, accelerated stress testing protocols and the durability of PEM stacks under variable load. At Velden Stackwerk GmbH she led the stack durability programme, which included the investigation of membrane and catalyst degradation under dynamic operation.

#### Selected publications

- Fenwick N., "Interface resistance at titanium porous transport layers in PEM electrolysis", *Journal of Electrochemical Energy Systems*, 2018.
- Fenwick N. and Abernethy J., "Dissolution of doped titania supports under dynamic load", *Electrochimica Acta Letters*, 2021.
- Fenwick N., "Accelerated stress testing of low-loading anodes: protocol design and interpretation", *Proceedings of the Hydrogen Technologies Conference*, 2019.
- Fenwick N. et al., "Thermal gradients in multi-cell electrolyser stacks", *International Journal of Hydrogen Energy*, 2017.

#### Professional memberships

Member, Royal Society of Chemistry. Member, regional hydrogen cluster standards working group.
