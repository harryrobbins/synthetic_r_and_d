## 2. Claim Notification

### 2.1 Position on notification

A Claim Notification Form has not been submitted with this claim. The Company's adviser reviewed the notification requirements for the 2025 accounting period and concluded that one was not required.

The Company made an R&D claim in respect of its 2024 accounting period. That claim was submitted by the Company's previous adviser and was accepted without enquiry.
