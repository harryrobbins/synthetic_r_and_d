### 1.1 Purpose of this claim

Quillon Hydrogen Ltd ("Quillon", "the Company") designs and manufactures proton exchange membrane (PEM) electrolyser stacks for the production of green hydrogen. The Company was founded in 2021 and employs 23 people at its headquarters in Tyneford. It operates a 250 kW demonstrator pilot plant at the Seal Sands East chemical cluster site.

During the period the Company carried out two research and development projects. The principal project, QH-LOWIR, seeks to reduce the anode iridium loading in PEM stacks below 0.2 mg/cm² while holding degradation below 10 µV/h at 2 A/cm². The second project, QH-DYNA, addresses stack-level thermal management under the dynamic load profiles that renewable power sources impose on electrolysers.

Both projects sought to resolve scientific and technological uncertainties that could not be resolved by a competent professional working in the field from knowledge that was readily available at the start of the period. This claim is made on the basis of that work.
