## 2. Claim Notification Form

A Claim Notification Form (CNF) is not included with this claim.

Excelcion is not a first-time claimant. The company has claimed R&D relief in each accounting period in recent years, and the most recent enquiry concerned the FY2022-23 claim, which was closed in 2024 with a modest adjustment (see section 4). Because the company has an established claim history, the R&D projects in this period were already within the company's existing reporting arrangements, and the Growth Fund reference numbers were in place before the period began. The projects are therefore reported in this Additional Information Form and in the technical narrative rather than through a separate notification.

The four projects are continuing programmes, not new projects started in this period. P1 (GF-25-014) was registered with the Growth Fund in February 2025, before the accounting period began, and its work continued through the period. P2 (GF-24-031) was registered in 2024 and is a continuation of work on the same surrogate-model programme. P3 and P4 are described in section 5 and were recorded against Growth Fund and Excelcion 2030 codes throughout the period.

Excelcion's position is that a CNF is not required for this claim. If the reviewing officer would find a notification helpful, the company will provide the Growth Fund papers listed in the evidence index in section 7, and the Keystone code list for the period, on request. The Head of Research and Innovation, Scott Lam, is the named contact for any questions about the project records.
