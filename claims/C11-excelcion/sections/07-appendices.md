### Appendix A: Competent professional CVs

#### A.1 Dermot McGregor, Chief Digital and Information Officer (P1 and P4)

Dermot McGregor has been Chief Digital and Information Officer of Excelcion Limited since January 2025. He leads the firm's digital, data, information and technology functions, including the Applied AI and Automation team, the Digital Delivery team and the Keystone and Lighthouse platforms, and he sponsors the Excelcion 2030 digital programme.

Before joining Excelcion he spent eleven years in digital engineering leadership roles in the infrastructure sector, most recently as Director of Digital Engineering at Ashdown Gorse Infrastructure, where he led the introduction of model-based design and automated checking into structural and civil design practice. Earlier he worked as a structural engineer on transport and water schemes, and he is a chartered engineer and a member of the institution for civil engineering.

He has written on the governance of AI tools in engineering practice and on the professional responsibilities of engineers who use them. He is the competent professional for P1 (EDITH, GF-25-014) and for P4 (common project data model and automated project set-up).

#### A.2 Ian Ritchie, Principal Data Scientist (P2)

Ian Ritchie has been Principal Data Scientist at Excelcion Limited since 2019 and leads the firm's data science practice. He is responsible for the statistical and machine-learning methods used across the firm's projects, and he supervises the data science staff who work with the Water and Digital teams.

He holds a doctorate in applied mathematics. Before joining Excelcion he worked as a research scientist on numerical methods for free-surface flow at a university research group, and as a data scientist in a regional water utility, where he built models of network performance from sensor and inspection records. He has published on the application of graph-based methods to scientific computing and on the limits of data-driven models in engineering practice.

He is the author of the Insights essay What data science can and cannot do for an engineer, published on 12 November 2025. He is the competent professional for P2 (GF-24-031).

#### A.3 Kenneth Norris, Associate Director, Buildings Mechanical Engineering (P3)

Kenneth Norris is Associate Director for Buildings Mechanical Engineering at Excelcion Limited, where he leads the group of mechanical engineers that designs building services for data centres, laboratories and healthcare buildings. He has been with the firm for fourteen years.

He is a chartered mechanical engineer with more than twenty years' experience of building services. He has worked on the thermal design of data centres and laboratories and on the selection of cooling strategies for high-density buildings. Before joining Excelcion he worked as a mechanical design engineer on commercial and industrial buildings, and he has contributed to professional guidance on heat rejection for dense computing.

He is the author of the Insights article Cooling dense computing: choose the strategy before the building, published on 10 June 2025. He is the competent professional for P3.
