## 4. Company background

### 4.1 History and purpose

Excelcion was founded in 1963 in Manchester by Edith Marlowe, a structural engineer, and Tomasz Reddish, a civil engineer. The firm began with a small practice of bridge and building designers and grew steadily through the decades that followed, taking on water, transport and energy work as the country's infrastructure programmes expanded. The trading company, Excelcion Limited, was formed on 11 February 1974 as Marlowe Reddish & Partners Limited and was renamed Excelcion Limited in 1999.

Excelcion describes itself as an independent, employee-owned firm of engineers, designers, planners and advisers working on the built environment. Its tagline, "Engineering places that last", reflects a long-standing commitment to designs that remain safe, practical and useful for the people who live with them over many decades. The firm's current strategy, Excelcion 2030, sets out how it intends to improve the quality of its engineering, reduce the carbon of the places it designs and make its digital working practices more effective. Its net-zero programme, Zero by Design, applies the same principles to the carbon content of designs from the first concept sketch.

The firm's values are technical pride, collegiate working and cautious judgement. Engineers are expected to check their work, to ask for advice early and to put professional responsibility ahead of commercial pressure. Peer review is standard on design work above a set risk threshold, and the firm's Heads of Discipline are accountable for the quality of work in their networks.

### 4.2 Group structure and ownership

Excelcion Limited is the UK trading company and the claimant. Its parent is Excelcion Group Limited, formerly Marlowe Reddish Group Limited, which was formed on 1 June 1987. The group is ultimately owned by the Excelcion Trust, an employee ownership trust established in 1999 and held through Excelcion Trustees Limited. Under the trust arrangements, the benefit of the firm's profits is shared among employees, and the trust's trustees are independent of the executive team.

The group's principal subsidiaries are as follows.

- **Tarnbeck Hydro Ltd**, which provides hydropower and water-resource engineering services.
- **Tarnbeck Modelling Services Ltd**, a hydraulic and environmental modelling business acquired in 2018. It provides modelling staff to Excelcion projects and is a connected party for the purposes of this claim.
- **Corrowa Rail Partners Pty Ltd**, a rail engineering and advisory business acquired in 2021 and operating in Australia.
- **Halvorsen Pike Engineers Inc.**, a structural and civil engineering firm in the United States, acquired on 1 November 2024. Its integration into the group is being carried out under a programme known internally as Project Meridian. It is a connected party for the purposes of this claim.

The group employs about 5,980 people across 28 offices in 17 countries. About 3,100 of those people work in the United Kingdom and Ireland. Group revenue was £648m in the financial year 2024/25 and is expected to be about £690m for 2025/26, with an operating margin in the range of 5% to 8%. The firm's work is divided across Transport (31%), Water (16%), Cities and Property (15%), Energy (13%), Social Infrastructure (10%), Science, Industry and Technology (9%) and Advisory (6%).

### 4.4 The Growth Fund and research governance

Excelcion established the Growth Fund in April 2019 to pay for research and development that the firm would not otherwise undertake on a commercial basis. The fund is governed through a panel of senior leaders and is managed by the Head of Research and Innovation, Scott Lam, who has funded research across the firm since 2019. Each project is supported by a paper setting out its objectives, budget, technical plan and the people responsible for it. Growth Fund papers are assigned references in the form GF-YY-NNN. The two Growth Fund papers covered by this claim are GF-25-014 (EDITH) and GF-24-031 (flood forecasting surrogates).

Projects are reviewed at quarterly panel meetings. Progress is reported against the technical plan, and the panel can stop, redirect or extend a project. Costs are recorded to Keystone project codes linked to each Growth Fund paper, and the Group Tax team reviews the codes before each annual claim.

### 4.5 Systems and data

Keystone is the firm's cloud enterprise resource planning system. It went live in April 2021 and holds the project, financial and resourcing records for the group, together with timesheet entries recorded through the Hourglass timesheet module. Lighthouse, the firm's data platform, was introduced in 2023 and holds design, asset and project information for analysis. Ledgerline is the firm's legacy system and has been read-only since April 2021. It holds historical project records that are being migrated progressively to Keystone and Lighthouse under P4.

The R&D cost history for each project is produced by a nightly job, TAX_RD_EXTRACT, which reads the Keystone project codes designated for research and development and writes them to the tax reporting environment.

### 4.6 Digital and AI governance

In May 2025 the Chief Digital and Information Officer and the General Counsel issued guidance on the use of AI tools across the firm. The guidance sets out which client and project data may be used with these tools, the requirement that engineers check outputs before they are issued, and the rule that only approved tools may be used for firm work. It is the first policy issued under the Excelcion 2030 digital strategy.

The first cohort of engineers and project managers began using an approved AI assistant on 22 September 2025. A controlled internal pilot of the company's own engineering checking interface, called Ask Edith, ran alongside the assistant in the same cohort. The firm has an Applied AI and Automation team of 12 full-time equivalents who support the rollout, train users and maintain the prompt libraries and guidance. Licences for the approved assistant were ordered under procurement order PROC-2025-0918.

### 4.7 Claim history and enquiry

Excelcion claims R&D relief in each accounting period. The claim for FY2022-23 was subject to an HMRC enquiry, which was closed in 2024 with a modest adjustment. The firm accepted the outcome. The timesheet and code-recording arrangements described in section 6 apply to this claim and to the firm's claims in subsequent periods.

### 4.8 Facilities

The firm's head office is Marlowe House, 41 Fitzroy Square, London ZZ1T 6EX. The firm also operates offices in Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Bristol and Belfast. The research and innovation team works from the London office, and the immersion-cooling test rig for P3 is housed in a dedicated laboratory at the Manchester office. The data platform and the Keystone environment are hosted by their cloud providers in the United Kingdom.
