### Summary of the claim

Excelcion is an employee-owned firm of engineers, designers, planners and advisers working on the built environment. Its research and innovation activity is coordinated through the Growth Fund, which has supported research across the firm since April 2019. This claim covers the accounting period from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026 and sets out four projects that together reflect the firm's work to advance engineering methods, hydraulic forecasting, building services design and the way project data is held and used.

The four projects are described in full in section 5 of this pack. P1 and P2 are the main research projects. P1 concerns a deterministic checker that verifies structural and hydraulic calculations against design code clauses, and the rollout of the approved AI assistant to the first user cohort from 22 September 2025. P2 concerns surrogate models that reproduce the output of two-dimensional hydrodynamic flood models in seconds. P3 concerns boiling heat-transfer behaviour in dielectric fluids at high rack densities, and P4 concerns the data model and automation that underpin project set-up across the firm.

Staff costs are apportioned using averages of Hourglass timesheet bookings to the Keystone project codes designated for the claim. Other costs are identified from the same codes and are set out in section 6. The expenditure credit is calculated at 20% of total qualifying expenditure. The supporting evidence, competent professional statements and timesheet extract are in section 7.
