### Summary of the claim

Lantern Lane Joinery Ltd is a bespoke kitchen and furniture maker based on the Lantern Lane Industrial Estate in Wexcombe. The Company employs four people: two directors, a joiner and an apprentice joiner. Its turnover for the period was £640,000. The Company has never previously claimed research and development tax relief. It has engaged Quorlane R&D Partners Ltd to prepare and submit this claim with its Corporation Tax return (CT600) for the accounting period ended 31 March 2025.

Throughout the claim period the Company sought to achieve an appreciable improvement in the overall knowledge and capability in its field, resolving uncertainties that could not be readily deduced by a competent professional working in the field, in accordance with paragraphs 3, 6 and 13 of the DSIT Guidelines.

Three projects are claimed. Each addresses a practical problem that the Company encountered in the course of its bespoke work for domestic and commercial clients.

1. **Development of an innovative concealed-hinge bespoke larder system.** The Company set out to design a larder unit that would be sold to clients as a distinctive and reliable product, using a new internal layout built around commercially available concealed hinges.
2. **Advanced CNC toolpath optimisation for hardwood components.** The Company set out to develop reliable toolpath strategies for hardwood components on a newly acquired CNC router, so that bespoke parts could be produced to a consistent standard.
3. **Sustainable finishing techniques.** The Company set out to evaluate finishing systems that would meet client expectations for appearance and durability while reducing the environmental impact of its finishing process.

The claim covers staff costs of £132,814 for four employees and directors, who have been apportioned to the projects on the basis set out in the Costs section of this pack. Other qualifying expenditure of £91,700 comprises consumables, software and related costs. The total qualifying expenditure is £224,514.

The Company is profitable and therefore claims under the merged scheme. The expenditure credit at 20% amounts to £44,903. After Corporation Tax at 25% the net benefit to the Company is £33,677.
