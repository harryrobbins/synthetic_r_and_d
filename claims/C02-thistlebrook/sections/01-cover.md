### Claim summary

Thistlebrook Precision Engineering Ltd has been a family-owned precision machining business since 1987 and has, since 2019, developed an additive manufacturing capability based on laser powder-bed fusion (LPBF) of titanium alloys. This claim covers the company's research and development in the accounting period ended 30 September 2025. The principal project, THINFIN (internal reference TPE-2024-07), addresses the distortion and build failure of thin-walled Ti-6Al-4V plate-fin heat exchangers produced without internal supports. A secondary workstream on powder reuse limits for the same alloy under high recoat-frequency builds forms part of the same project and is not separately costed.

The company faced a technical uncertainty that existing commercial build-simulation tools could not resolve: whether distortion in 0.35 mm walls could be predicted and compensated to a tolerance sufficient for aerospace heat-exchanger use. The advance achieved is a combined thermo-mechanical calibration and scan-strategy method that delivers dimensional accuracy of ±0.07 mm on 0.35 mm walls over a 180 mm build height, with no internal supports. This is an advance in the field of additive manufacturing process science and is set out in full in the technical narrative.
