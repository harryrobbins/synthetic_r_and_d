## 4. Company Background

### 4.1 History and business

Brackenmoor Groundworks Ltd was incorporated in 2009 by Darren and Paul Kettlewell, who had worked together as groundworkers for more than a decade before founding the business. The Company began as a small enabling and groundworks contractor serving housebuilders and private clients in South Yorkshire. Over the following fifteen years it has grown into a regional contractor delivering site preparation, drainage, ground remediation, retaining structures and basement construction for residential, commercial and light civil projects.

The Company is headquartered at Brackenmoor Yard, Clough Lane, Holmfield, Sheffield ZZ35 8RW. The yard provides an office for the management team, a workshop, a secure plant and materials store and a welfare facility for site staff. The Company operates its own fleet of excavators, dumpers, dewatering pumps and support plant, supplemented by hired plant where demand requires.

The Company's work is typically let under JCT-form and bespoke contracts on a fixed-price or re-measured basis. Its largest residential basement to date is the Hallam Road project described in this claim, which was delivered under a JCT design-and-build variant at a fixed price of £1.38 million. The Company has since carried out several smaller basement and underpinning schemes using the same site management arrangements.

### 4.2 Ownership and group structure

The issued share capital of the Company is held equally by Darren Kettlewell and Paul Kettlewell. Both are directors, and both are actively engaged in the management of the business. The Company has no parent undertaking, holds no subsidiaries and is not part of a wider group. No shareholder is a company that would make the Company connected with any other claimant for the purposes of the research and development scheme.

The Company has not previously made a claim for research and development relief. This is its first claim.

### 4.3 Funding

The Company has funded its operations from retained earnings and from a revolving credit facility provided by a regional bank. The facility is used for working capital and for the acquisition of plant under asset finance agreements. The Company has no grant funding, no external equity investment and no loan from a director that is not repayable on normal commercial terms.

Funding for the Hallam Road project came from the client under the design-and-build contract, paid in stage payments against certified valuations. Cash flow for the project was managed through the Company's existing facility and was not dependent on any external grant or subsidy.

### 4.4 Staff and organisation

At the end of the accounting period the Company employed 31 staff. These comprised a management team of two directors, a contracts and estimating function, a site engineering function, a plant and workshop team and a body of groundworkers.

The Company's management structure is simple and direct. Darren Kettlewell leads the business overall, with particular responsibility for health and safety, commercial strategy and the Company's relationship with its key clients. Paul Kettlewell, as Contracts Manager, is responsible for programme and delivery across live contracts. The site engineering function, led by Amira Saleh as Site Engineer, prepares temporary works designs, monitors ground movement and manages site-based technical queries. The estimating function, led by Jade Holroyd as Estimator, prices new work and maintains cost records for live contracts.

Site operatives are employed on a permanent basis and deployed across the Company's live sites. Where a project requires specialist skills, the Company engages subcontractors or specialist suppliers under written agreements.

The Company's staff who work on the projects in this claim are identified in Section 6. Each individual has been assessed on the basis of their role on the relevant projects, as set out in the methodology.

### 4.5 Facilities

Brackenmoor Yard holds the Company's administrative, plant and workshop facilities. The Company maintains a site office on each live project, with a shared drive for drawings, a photographic record and a site diary. For the digital project, described in the technical narrative, the Company used cloud-hosted services and mobile devices issued to site supervisors and to a subset of operatives.

The Company does not maintain a dedicated laboratory or test facility. Testing of concrete and soil samples is carried out by accredited external laboratories under commercial agreements, and monitoring instrumentation is installed and read by the Company's site engineering team.

### 4.6 Health, safety and quality

The Company operates an integrated management system covering quality, health and safety and environmental practice. Its procedures include method statements for every basement and excavation operation, risk assessments reviewed at each design stage, and a formal permit-to-dig process. The Company's record for the accounting period is consistent with that of an established contractor of its size.
