The costs in this section relate to the accounting period 1 August 2024 to 31 July 2025. The Company's staff costs are for employees on its payroll throughout the period. ### 6.6 Methodology for staff apportionment

The proportion of each individual's time attributed to the research and development activities was established at a workshop held at Brackenmoor Yard on 14 January 2026. The workshop was attended by Darren Kettlewell, Paul Kettlewell, Amira Saleh and Jade Holroyd. The adviser from Quorlane R&D Partners Ltd attended to record the outcome and to explain the eligibility criteria that apply to staff costs. The workshop record is at evidence item BMG-WS-01 in Section 7.

The directors' apportionments were agreed by reference to the following information, which was available for each individual and each period:

- the site diary entries for each project, showing the activities undertaken on each site on each working day;
- the monitoring log for Hallam Road, recording the inclinometer and groundwater readings, the triggers reached and the decisions taken in response;
- the request for information (RFI) register, which records each occasion on which the design or the ground differed from the assumptions in the method statement, and the people who responded;
- the site photographs taken at each stage of the Hallam Road works and during the P2 pilot; and
- the client contract and the associated programme, which set out the stages of the works and the people responsible for each.

For the directors, the apportionment reflects their involvement in the design review, the monitoring decisions, the propping and dewatering sequence and the supervision of the pilot. Darren Kettlewell's apportionment is 70 per cent, reflecting his direction of the technical work on both projects. Paul Kettlewell's apportionment is 60 per cent, reflecting his responsibility for programme and for the site supervision that governed the monitoring and release process. Both apportionments also take account of the time spent on the commercial and contractual management of the works, which has been excluded from the figure.

For the Site Engineer, Amira Saleh, the apportionment of 80 per cent reflects her role in preparing the temporary works sequence, reviewing the inclinometer readings, managing the RFI register and leading the survey and BIM workflow for Project P2.

For the Estimator, Jade Holroyd, the apportionment of 50 per cent reflects the time spent on the cost and programme implications of the design changes recorded in the RFI register, and on the configuration of the cost codes used in the P2 site diary.

For the site operatives, the apportionment of 20 per cent across the 11 full-time equivalent posts reflects the proportion of working time spent on the monitoring installation and reading, the excavation and propping stages, the dewatering arrangement and the entry of site records into the P2 system. The workshop agreed that this proportion was consistent with the operatives' deployment across the period, and that it was a reasonable and conservative estimate of their time on the research activities.

### 6.7 Methodology for other qualifying expenditure

**Subcontract.** The Company has claimed 65 per cent of the fee for the basement design work carried out by Pennine Structural Consultants, which totals £84,000. Pennine Structural Consultants is a UK-based structural engineering practice with no connection to the Company. The proportion of 65 per cent reflects the share of the design development that addressed the uncertainties identified in Section 5.6, namely the stability of the party wall, the unknown ground conditions and the propping sequence in a high water table. The remainder of the design work was routine structural design to established practice and is excluded. The design development outputs and the invoice are listed in Section 7.

**Consumables.** The concrete, sheet piles and rebar were incorporated into the Hallam Road basement. Each item was used in the construction of the retaining structure and the basement, and each was consumed in the course of the works. The total of £212,000 is taken from the supplier invoices and the Company's purchase ledger, and is attributed to the project in full because the materials were used only for the basement, which is the subject of Project P1.

**Plant hire.** Plant hire of £46,000 covers the excavators, dewatering pumps and propping equipment hired for the Hallam Road works during the period. Plant hire is treated as a consumable because the plant is used up in the course of the works, and the hire charges are wholly attributable to the research activities on Project P1.

### 6.8 Relief computation

The qualifying expenditure of £619,596 is the sum of the staff costs of £306,996 and the other qualifying expenditure of £312,600. The expenditure credit at 20 per cent is £123,919. The net benefit after notional corporation tax at 25 per cent is £92,939.

The Company's turnover for the accounting period was £11,400,000 and its headcount was 31. The Company is profit-making in the period, and the claim is made under the merged scheme on that basis.
