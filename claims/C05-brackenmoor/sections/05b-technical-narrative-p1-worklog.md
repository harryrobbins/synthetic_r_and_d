## 5. Technical Narrative: Project P1 Work Log, Outcomes and Boundaries

### 5.9 Project P1: Chronological work log

The following log records the principal iterations of the P1 work within the accounting period. Monitoring readings are given in millimetres of lateral displacement at the inclinometer nearest the party wall. Each entry is drawn from the project log, the monitoring log and the RFI register, which are listed in the evidence index at Section 7.

**August 2024: pre-start investigation and baseline monitoring**

- 1 August 2024. Site mobilised. Existing finishes and the condition of the party wall were recorded by photograph and by a written schedule agreed with the neighbouring owner. Trial pits were excavated along the party wall line to confirm the depth of the existing foundation.
- 12 August 2024. Trial pit results were reviewed. The foundation depth was found to be shallower than the desk study had indicated. The design team was notified, and RFI BMG-RFI-004 was raised to confirm the assumed bearing depth for the party wall.
- 20 August 2024. Inclinometers I1 to I4 were installed in the ground beside the party wall. Groundwater standpipes GW1 to GW3 were installed within the excavation footprint.
- 20 August to 6 September 2024. Baseline readings were taken on alternate days. The readings showed a normal diurnal pattern of small movement, with a range of 0.8 mm across the period. Groundwater levels varied seasonally by up to 0.6 m.

**September 2024: design review and temporary works sequence**

- 9 September 2024. The design-development log was reviewed. The three uncertainties were confirmed in writing: party wall stability during excavation, unknown ground conditions, and propping sequence in a high water table. A monitoring response was attached to each.
- 16 September 2024. The first draft temporary works sequence was issued by the site engineering team. The draft proposed four excavation stages with propping at two levels. It was reviewed against the baseline readings and against the made ground data from the trial pits.
- 23 September 2024. The amber and red triggers were agreed with the client's design team and the party wall surveyor. Amber was set at 10 mm lateral movement at any inclinometer, and red at 15 mm. A movement rate trigger of 2 mm in any 24-hour period was also agreed.
- 30 September 2024. The draft sequence was revised after the designer's review. The revision introduced a short pause at formation of the first propping level to allow the response of the party wall to be measured before the second stage began. RFI BMG-RFI-009 recorded the change.

**October 2024: secant piling**

- 7 October 2024. Piling commenced. Sheet piles were driven to form the guide wall and temporary capping. Concrete was placed to the secant piles by tremie method, with reinforcement cages installed to the design.
- 14 October 2024. Pile records for the first panel were reviewed. Cover and verticality were within the tolerances specified. Pile head levels were recorded and compared with the design.
- 21 October 2024. The second group of piles was installed. A concrete sample was taken for testing at 28 days. The inclinometers recorded movement of 1.4 mm during piling, within the amber trigger.
- 28 October 2024. Piling completed on the north and east elevations. Inclinometer readings were reviewed and the sequence for excavation was confirmed for the first stage.

**November 2024: first excavation stage and propping**

- 4 November 2024. Excavation to the first stage commenced. The excavation was taken down in lifts of 0.6 m, with monitoring readings taken after each lift.
- 11 November 2024. Inclinometer I2 recorded lateral movement of 4 mm at the first lift. The reading was within the amber trigger. The site engineering team reviewed the reading and recommended the second lift proceed as planned.
- 18 November 2024. The first propping level was installed. The propping sequence was recorded, including the time between excavation and installation, and the load applied to each prop. RFI BMG-RFI-014 recorded a minor change to the bearing detail at the propping connection.
- 25 November 2024. Movement after propping was monitored for seven days. The reading at inclinometer I2 reduced to 3 mm and stabilised.

**December 2024: second stage and dewatering**

- 2 December 2024. The second excavation stage commenced. Dewatering was started at two wellpoints, with pumping rates recorded daily.
- 9 December 2024. Groundwater levels in standpipes GW1 and GW2 fell by 0.9 m following the start of dewatering. The change was recorded and compared with the predicted drawdown. The party wall recorded no change beyond the normal pattern.
- 16 December 2024. Inclinometer I3 recorded 6 mm movement after the second lift. The site engineering team reviewed the readings with the Contracts Manager and advised a reduced lift height of 0.4 m for the remainder of the stage.
- 23 December 2024. The second excavation stage was completed to the agreed level. The second propping level was installed on 30 December 2024.

**January 2025: water ingress and revised dewatering**

- 6 January 2025. Water inflow was observed at the south-west corner of the excavation, at a point where the made ground contained a former drainage trench. The inflow was recorded, the excavation was stopped at that corner, and photographs were taken.
- 8 January 2025. RFI BMG-RFI-031 was raised. The inflow was considered in the context of the groundwater model. The designer confirmed that the inflow was consistent with a localised pocket of more permeable material, and recommended a revised wellpoint layout at the south-west corner.
- 13 January 2025. The revised wellpoint layout was installed and tested. Pumping rates were recorded hourly for 48 hours. The inflow was controlled and the excavation was resumed.
- 20 January 2025. Lateral movement at inclinometer I4, the nearest instrument to the south-west corner, reached 9 mm. This was within the amber trigger. Readings were reviewed daily until the movement rate reduced.
- 27 January 2025. Movement rate at all inclinometers returned to below 1 mm in any 24-hour period. The third excavation stage was approved to proceed.

**February 2025: third stage and formation**

- 3 February 2025. The third excavation stage commenced with lifts of 0.4 m. The sequence for the third propping level was reviewed against the monitoring record.
- 10 February 2025. The third propping level was installed. The propping load was recorded for each prop and compared with the design load. Two props were re-tensioned to bring the load into line with the design, and the re-tensioning was recorded in the project log.
- 17 February 2025. Inclinometer I2 recorded lateral movement of 7 mm, the highest reading recorded since the January 2025 water inflow. The reading was within the amber trigger. The designer was informed and no change to the sequence was required.
- 24 February 2025. Excavation reached formation level. Formation was inspected and the foundation details were confirmed by the designer.

**March 2025: base slab and monitoring at formation**

- 3 March 2025. Blinding and the base slab reinforcement were installed. The slab was poured in two pours on 10 and 11 March 2025.
- 17 March 2025. Monitoring continued at formation. The readings showed the party wall had settled to a stable position, with movement of less than 1 mm in the preceding two weeks.
- 31 March 2025. The ground beam and lower wall elements were constructed. Concrete cubes were tested at 7 days and met the specified strength.

**April and May 2025: structure and backfill**

- 7 April 2025. Lower wall construction commenced. The connection between the wall and the base slab was inspected and recorded.
- 28 April 2025. Ground floor slab was poured.
- 12 May 2025. Structure was completed to the first floor level. Monitoring readings were reviewed and no movement beyond the amber trigger was recorded during the structural works.
- 26 May 2025. Backfill commenced against the basement walls, placed in layers with compaction tests recorded for each layer.

**June and July 2025: propping removal and completion**

- 9 June 2025. The removal sequence for the propping was agreed with the designer. Propping was to be removed in reverse order, with monitoring at each stage.
- 16 June 2025. The first prop level was removed. Movement at all inclinometers was recorded for seven days after removal. The largest movement recorded was 2 mm.
- 23 June 2025. The second and third prop levels were removed. Readings were taken at intervals of 24 hours, and the peak lateral movement recorded during the removal was 3 mm at inclinometer I2.
- 7 July 2025. Monitoring continued after removal of all propping. Readings showed the party wall had returned to a stable position, with no movement beyond the normal pattern.
- 25 July 2025. Works to the basement were completed within the accounting period. The final monitoring readings were recorded and the monitoring instruments were retained for the post-completion review.

### 5.10 Project P1: Outcomes and boundaries

**Outcomes.** The basement was completed within the accounting period, with the party wall remaining within the amber trigger throughout the works. The peak lateral movement recorded in the monitoring log was 9 mm at inclinometer I4 in January 2025, within the amber trigger of 10 mm. The Company has recorded how the secant pile wall, the propping sequence and the dewatering arrangement behaved in made ground with a high water table, and how each responded when the ground conditions differed from the assumptions in the design.

Three outcomes are of particular significance to the advance. First, the Company established a monitoring-based release process for each excavation stage, which allowed the works to be controlled against measured performance rather than against fixed programme dates. Second, the Company identified the sensitivity of the party wall to lift height, and set a reduced lift height where the readings required it. Third, the Company identified a localised permeable pocket in the made ground, which the designer's revised wellpoint layout addressed.

The outcomes have been incorporated into the Company's temporary works procedures and are now applied to comparable basement projects.

**Start boundary.** The P1 work within the claim began on 1 August 2024, with the site mobilisation and the pre-start recording of the party wall. The design-development review, the monitoring installation and the baseline readings all fall within the accounting period.

**End boundary.** The P1 work within the claim ended on 25 July 2025, with the completion of the basement and the final monitoring readings. Subsequent maintenance and defects work is not included in this claim.
