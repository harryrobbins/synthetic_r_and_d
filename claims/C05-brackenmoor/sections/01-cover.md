### Claim summary

Brackenmoor Groundworks Ltd is a groundworks and basement contractor founded in 2009 and owned by the brothers Darren and Paul Kettlewell. The Company employs 31 staff and reported turnover of £11.4 million for the period. This is the Company's first claim for research and development relief.

The claim covers two projects. Project P1, "Hallam Road deep basement: innovative retaining solution in challenging ground conditions", addressed the construction of a two-storey basement beneath a Victorian terrace with a high water table and made ground. The Company's enhanced capability in deep basement construction was advanced through a secant pile retaining solution with a temporary propping sequence, resolving uncertainties about the stability of the adjacent party wall and about unknown ground conditions. Project P2, "Digital transformation of site operations", evaluated and integrated a cloud-based site diary application, drone survey capture and a building information modelling (BIM) viewer into day-to-day site operations, resolving uncertainty about whether site teams would adopt the technology.
