#### 5.4.7 Results of the challenge-testing programme

The results of the three challenge runs are summarised below. Counts are given as log colony forming units per gram of product. The acceptance criterion for the programme was that the *Listeria monocytogenes* count should not increase by more than 1.0 log over the full shelf life at 8 degrees Celsius, which is the abuse temperature used in the protocol.

**Run C1 (modified-atmosphere packaging only, no antimicrobial).** At 8 degrees Celsius the count rose from 2.0 log at day 0 to 3.6 log at day 12, an increase of 1.6 log. The run did not meet the acceptance criterion and was used to confirm that packaging alone was insufficient.

**Run C2 (modified-atmosphere packaging and cultured dextrose at 0.4% by weight).** At 8 degrees Celsius the count rose from 2.1 log at day 0 to 2.8 log at day 12, an increase of 0.7 log. The run met the acceptance criterion. Sensory assessment noted a faint tang from day 10 onwards, which the sensory panel judged to be above the preferred level for the braised product.

**Run C3 (modified-atmosphere packaging and cultured dextrose at 0.3% by weight, with revised water activity).** The results of the final run are set out in Table 5.4.7 below.

| Sampling point (8 degrees Celsius) | *L. monocytogenes* (log cfu/g) | Water activity | pH |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | 2.1 | 0.979 | 5.4 |
| Day 5 | 2.2 | 0.978 | 5.4 |
| Day 9 | 2.4 | 0.977 | 5.3 |
| Day 12 | 2.9 | 0.976 | 5.3 |

Run C3 showed an increase of 0.8 log at day 12, within the acceptance criterion. Water activity was held between 0.976 and 0.979 throughout, below the specification limit of 0.985, and pH remained between 5.3 and 5.4. At 4 degrees Celsius the *Listeria* count changed by less than 0.2 log over the full twelve-day period. Sensory assessment of the run recorded no tang at day 0 and only a slight, acceptable note at day 12.

Counts for *Listeria* were confirmed by the laboratory using a validated enumeration method, and the laboratory's report for the run is held at HR-EV-052. The Company's trial book records the sensory panel scores for each sampling point at HR-LB-0441 to HR-LB-0446.

#### 5.4.8 Outcome of project P3

The outcome of project P3 is a validated shelf life of twelve days for the chilled braised product, achieved through modified-atmosphere packaging at 60% carbon dioxide and 40% nitrogen, cultured dextrose at 0.3% by weight, and a revised water activity target. The Company has issued a revised product specification covering packaging, recipe and shelf life, and the product has been produced to that specification since March 2025.

The uncertainty over whether the combination would control *Listeria monocytogenes* over twelve days was resolved by run C3. The uncertainty over flavour and texture was resolved through the sensory panel, which found the product acceptable at the revised cultured dextrose level. The uncertainty over the optimal antimicrobial concentration was resolved by comparing runs C2 and C3, which established 0.3% as the lowest level that met the acceptance criterion with an acceptable flavour.

The Company's view is that the outcome represents a material advance in its knowledge of how to apply modified-atmosphere packaging and natural antimicrobials to a braised, water-rich chilled product. Before the programme, the Company had no validated data on the behaviour of *Listeria* in its own products, and no means of predicting the effect of a given antimicrobial level on a given recipe. The validated data and the associated protocol now allow the Company to take further products through the same process with a defined method.

The Company acknowledges that the underlying techniques, namely modified-atmosphere packaging, refrigeration and the use of cultured dextrose, are established in the chilled food sector. The advance lies in the application to this product, the determination of the operating window, and the validation of the outcome against the Company's own acceptance criteria.
