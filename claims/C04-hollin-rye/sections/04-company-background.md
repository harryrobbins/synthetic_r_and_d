## 4. Company Background

### 4.1 History and business

Hollin & Rye Kitchens Ltd was incorporated in 2016 by Isla Hollin and Marcus Rye to develop a modern British restaurant concept built around seasonal produce, slow cookery and a strong sense of place. The first site opened on Lowgate Yard in Leeds in 2017. The Company has since grown to three restaurant sites in Leeds, York and Harrogate, which together serve around 2,400 covers in a typical week.

The restaurants offer a short à la carte menu and a seasonal tasting menu. The tasting menu is the Company's principal creative platform and is changed four times a year, in line with the seasons. Each menu cycle is developed in the Leeds development kitchen by the executive chef, the head chefs and the kitchen brigade, and is then rolled out to the York and Harrogate sites.

In September 2024 the Company launched H&R At Home, a chilled retail range of ready-to-heat braised dishes, soups and sauces. The range is currently sold through two regional delis and through the Company's own restaurant takeaway counters. It is produced in the Leeds commissary, which sits alongside the development kitchen.

### 4.2 Turnover and profitability

The Company's turnover for the accounting period ending 31 March 2025 was £6.2 million. The Company was profitable in the period and in each of the two preceding periods. Profit before tax for the period was approximately £0.44 million. Revenue is derived predominantly from restaurant covers, supplemented by private dining and, from September 2024, retail sales.

The Company is not part of a group. It has no subsidiaries, holds no interest in any other undertaking of more than a minor size, and has no partner or linked enterprises for the purposes of the SME test.

The directors are Isla Hollin (Managing Director) and Marcus Rye (Executive Chef and Director). The Company is managed day to day by the Managing Director, who has responsibility for finance, property, operations and the overall strategic direction of the business. The Executive Chef leads culinary direction across all three sites.

### 4.4 Funding

The Company is funded from retained profits and a Business Growth Loan of £150,000 taken out in 2023 with a high-street lender, which was used to fund the refurbishment of the Harrogate restaurant. The Company has not received any grant, subsidy or state aid in respect of the projects described in this claim. It has not received any venture capital or external equity.

### 4.5 Staff

The Company employed 74 people at 31 March 2025, equivalent to approximately 63 full-time equivalents. Staff are organised into front-of-house, kitchen and central teams.

The kitchen is led at each site by a head chef, supported by a sous chef and a line team. Across the three sites the kitchen brigade comprises 14 full-time equivalent sous and line chefs. The Leeds head chef, Tobias Wren, has been with the Company since 2018. The York head chef, Carmen Ruiz, joined in 2019 from a previous role as sous chef in a city centre restaurant. The Harrogate site is led by a site chef reporting to the Managing Director.

The Company engaged Ella Brandt, a food technologist, on a fixed-term contract in April 2024. Her contract was converted to a permanent role in January 2025. Ms Brandt holds a BSc in Food Science and has previously worked in the chilled food sector on product development and shelf-life programmes. She is responsible for food safety documentation, supplier specifications and the technical aspects of the retail range.

The executive chef, Marcus Rye, has 25 years of culinary experience. He trained in professional kitchens across the north of England and in London, and was head chef of a restaurant that held a Bib Gourmand award from a national restaurant guide before joining the Company as a co-founder. He has no formal scientific qualifications. His professional expertise is in cookery, flavour and kitchen practice, and the Company regards him as a recognised expert in culinary science within its sector.

### 4.6 Facilities

The Company operates the following facilities.

**Leeds development kitchen.** A purpose-built development kitchen at Lowgate Yard, equipped with a range of combi ovens, induction hobs, a dedicated development pass, and a tasting room seating twelve. The development kitchen is used for all menu development and is where the trials described in section 5 were carried out.

**Fermentation room.** A temperature-controlled fermentation room in the Leeds development kitchen, with a walk-in cold room and a dedicated fermentation chamber, installed in July 2024. The chamber allows controlled temperature and humidity conditions for koji, garum and lacto-fermented products.

**Leeds commissary.** A food production facility on the same site, used to produce the H&R At Home range. It has a modified-atmosphere packaging machine, a chilled dispatch area and a documented food safety management system. The Company does not operate a microbiology laboratory on site and uses external accredited laboratories for microbiological testing.

**Restaurant sites.** York and Harrogate each have a full kitchen with prep areas, cold storage and a dishwash area. Both sites receive menu development outputs from Leeds and implement them following written specifications.

### 4.7 Food safety and quality

The Company operates a documented food safety management system based on hazard analysis principles and reviewed each year by the Managing Director and the food technologist. The system covers supplier approval, temperature control, allergen management and traceability. Retail products are produced under a written specification covering ingredients, process parameters, packaging and shelf life.

### 4.8 Related activity

The Company's activities are concentrated in restaurant operation, private dining and the H&R At Home retail range. It does not undertake contract manufacturing for third parties, and it has no research collaborations other than the laboratory relationship described in project P3.
